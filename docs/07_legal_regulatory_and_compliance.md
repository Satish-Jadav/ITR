AUTHORIZATION FOR DIRECT IN-APP e-FILING AND STATUTORY ASSISTANCE

By proceeding with Portal Sync, you acknowledge and authorize that:

Client-Side Utility Architecture: This application connects directly from your mobile handset to the official Income Tax Department (ITD) e-Filing gateway (https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices) via secure hardware TLS. Taxpayers can review return schedules, authenticate sessions, upload CBDT JSON packages, and execute UIDAI Aadhaar OTP e-verification completely within the app without ever needing to open an external web browser.
Direct Portal Connection: When you authenticate via OTP on https://eportal.incometax.gov.in, the network connection is established directly between your mobile device and the official Income Tax Department servers.

Session tokens exist strictly in volatile RAM and are cryptographically zero-wiped within 500ms of verification completion per DPDP Act 2023 Section 12(3).

3.2 Black Money Act (Schedule FA) Foreign Asset Mandatory Alert
Triggered whenever foreign equity/RSUs/ESPPs or foreign bank accounts are detected in Phase 3.

MANDATORY FOREIGN ASSET DISCLOSURE ALERT — BLACK MONEY ACT, 2015

We detected foreign equity holdings / foreign income (RSUs, ESPPs, or foreign custodial accounts). Under the Income-tax Act, 1961, resident taxpayers (ROR) holding any foreign asset at any time during the Calendar Year (January 1 to December 31) are statutorily barred from filing ITR-1 or ITR-4 and must complete Schedule FA in ITR-2/ITR-3.

Statutory Consequence (Sections 42 & 43 of the Black Money (Undisclosed Foreign Income and Assets) and Imposition of Tax Act, 2015): Failure to disclose foreign assets or furnishing inaccurate particulars in Schedule FA attracts a flat statutory penalty of ₹10,00,000 (Rupees Ten Lakhs) per assessment year, even if tax was already deducted at source by your employer in Form 16.

हमने विदेशी इक्विटी होल्डिंग्स / विदेशी आय (RSUs, ESPPs, या विदेशी बैंक खाते) का पता लगाया है। आयकर अधिनियम, 1961 के तहत, कैलेंडर वर्ष के दौरान किसी भी समय किसी विदेशी संपत्ति के धारक निवासी करदाताओं को ITR-1 या ITR-4 दाखिल करने से वैधानिक रूप से प्रतिबंधित किया गया है और उन्हें ITR-2/ITR-3 में अनुसूची FA (Schedule FA) भरना अनिवार्य है।

वैधानिक परिणाम (काला धन अधिनियम, 2015 की धारा 42 और 43): अनुसूची FA में विदेशी संपत्ति का खुलासा न करने या गलत विवरण प्रस्तुत करने पर प्रति कर निर्धारण वर्ष ₹10,00,000 (दस लाख रुपये) का अनिवार्य वैधानिक जुर्माना लगता है, भले ही नियोक्ता द्वारा फॉर्म 16 में स्रोत पर कर (TDS) पहले ही काट लिया गया हो।
